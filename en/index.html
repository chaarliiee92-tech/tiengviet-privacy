Tiếng Việt cho bé: privacy policy

Effective 28 November 2026 · app version 1.0

In short: the app collects no data. Everything you enter or record stays on your device only. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, purchases or third-party tools, and it never connects to the internet.

Who makes the app

Tiếng Việt cho bé is developed by a private individual as a family project for children learning Vietnamese. Contact: karel.nguyen@icloud.com.

What data the app collects

None. The developer receives no information about you or your child: no name, no age, no recordings and no information about how the app or the device is used.

What is stored on your device

  • The child and adult profiles you create (name, birth date to pick the zodiac companion, settings).
  • Learning progress and the family's notes on what the child has said.
  • Microphone recordings: the child's name and the companion's name that you record, short recordings made during games, and adults' voice notes. Game recordings and voice notes are deleted automatically after 7 days. Recorded names are kept until you delete them in Settings.

This data never leaves the device. The app does not send it anywhere and has no means to do so.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when a game first needs it (the child tries saying a word out loud). Sound is processed on the device only: the app detects that the child is speaking, but it does not convert speech to text and sends nothing. You can turn the microphone off at any time in iOS Settings; the app keeps working without the speaking tasks.

Backup and sharing

Only an adult can create a family backup or a card of the day, in the parent area and always at your request. You decide where the file is saved or who it is sent to (for example Files, AirDrop, Zalo); those services have their own policies.

Children

The app is made for children and is listed in the Kids category of the App Store. Child screens contain no external links, ads or purchases. Settings, sharing and backups sit behind a parental gate. Because the app neither collects nor shares personal data, no consent to data processing is required.

Deleting data

You can delete a child and all of their records in Settings → child → Delete (the app offers a backup first). Deleting the app from the device removes all of its data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date. Any data collection would require a new version of the app and would be described here in advance.

Support

  • Questions and bugs: write to karel.nguyen@icloud.com (English, Czech or Vietnamese). Please include your device and app version.
  • Parental gate: hold two fingers for 2 seconds, then solve the sum.
  • Moving to a new device: Settings → Family → Back up, transfer the file (for example via AirDrop) and choose Restore from file on the new device.
  • Voices: all voices are built into the app and work offline.
